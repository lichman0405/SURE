# Future team edition — explicitly out of v0.1

Personal users retain the core checking product.

Future team value may add:

- shared rules;
- GitHub/GitLab PR integration;
- merge gates;
- shared history;
- approvals;
- role/permission management;
- organization privacy/model policies;
- private deployment management;
- team reporting.

Do not remove core checking from the individual/open-source product merely to create commercial differentiation.
